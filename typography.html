Reading measure

Long-form text is capped near 64 characters per line and uses --leading-body.

Most packs fail in the same place: the hip belt. A belt that rides above the hip bones moves the load onto the shoulders within the first hour. Before you compare litres or fabrics, stand sideways in front of a mirror and check where the padding lands. Everything else on the spec sheet matters less than that one fit.
