Colour

Colour tokens are named for their job, not their hue. The same --color-primary is cobalt in one theme, blue in another and gold in a third; the components never know which.

Surfaces

Four steps from page background to the most raised fill. In light mode cards sit lighter than the page; in dark mode the steps climb toward lighter greys.
