Buttons

One .btn class with modifiers. Variants only swap three local variables (background, foreground, border), so a new variant is three lines of CSS.

Variants

Use one primary per view. Secondary for the alternative, soft and ghost for low-emphasis actions, danger only for actions that destroy data.

<button class="btn btn--primary">Save changes</button>
<button class="btn btn--secondary">Preview</button>

Sizes and shapes

States

Hover and press are handled in CSS. Busy buttons keep their width, hide the label and show a spinner; press the last one to see it.

With icons

Icon-only buttons need an aria-label.
